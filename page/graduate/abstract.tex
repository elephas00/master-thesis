\chapternonum{摘要}
随着云计算、大数据与人工智能应用的快速发展，数据存储与计算需求持续增长，推动数据中心规模不断扩大。固态硬盘作为关键存储介质，其可靠性与寿命利用效率直接影响业务连续性和运维成本。
针对这一应用场景，本文关注两个相互关联但侧重点不同的问题：
其一，故障预测问题，即在监控数据早期信息有限且噪声较多的条件下，如何在低误报约束下稳定识别潜在故障盘；
其二，寿命提升问题，即在统一寿命阈值策略难以刻画块间耐久差异的情况下，如何有效识别块的使用寿命，并在近零误报约束下尽可能准确地预测出块寿命，进而将预测结果转化为可执行策略以提高整体可写入量。围绕上述问题，本文开展了如下工作：

（1）围绕故障预测问题，基于阿里云公开 SMART 数据集开展 B 组近端突变前兆观察，提出混合标注策略抑制静默段标签噪声，
构造 $W$ 阶 滞后特征与加权突变特征相结合的短期时序突变特征，
并采用 RF、XGB、LGB 异构基学习器与 Attention 样本级融合构成集成模型；
实验按日历月时间顺序划分，以盘级 $F_{0.5}$ 为主指标在验证集上优化阈值。
在 MB1/MB2 上，异构集成三轮测试均值分别取得 $F_{0.5}{=}0.921$、TPR $0.738$ 与 $F_{0.5}{=}0.490$、TPR $0.187$，
且 FPR 维持在较低水平，验证了所提方法在低误报约束下的有效性。

（2）围绕寿命提升问题，基于块级错误比特计数（Fault Bit Count, FBC）构建寿命标签，并将块寿命预测问题转化为强块/弱块分类问题：对弱块采用更保守写入上限、对强块在安全边界内适度放宽阈值，以提升整体可写入量。实验结果显示，相比统一 PE 阈值策略，该方法可带来约 17\% 的累计写入量提升，验证了差异化写入策略的有效性。

（3）最后，基于本文提出的故障预测与寿命提升方法，设计并实现智慧固态硬盘运维软件，形成从预警生成到状态管理的闭环流程，并实现资产管理、日志管理、故障记录与预警处理等功能，验证了方法在工程场景中的可部署性与实用性。

综上，本文按“故障预测—寿命优化—系统落地”的结构形成了较完整的技术链路，在不显著增加误报风险的前提下提升了故障识别能力与存储资源利用效率，为数据中心固态硬盘的可靠性运维与成本优化提供了可行方法与实践参考。

\textbf{关键词}：固态硬盘；机器学习；故障预测；寿命提升；低误报率约束

\chapternonum{Abstract}

With the rapid development of cloud computing, big data, and artificial intelligence applications, storage and computing demands continue to grow, which drives the continuous expansion of data centers.
As a key storage medium, the reliability and lifetime utilization efficiency of solid-state drives (SSDs) directly affect service continuity and operation costs. 
In this context, this thesis focuses on two related but distinct problems:
 (1) for failure prediction, stably identifying potential faulty drives under low false-alarm constraints when early monitoring signals are weak and noisy; and 
 (2) for lifetime enhancement, identifying strong and weak blocks and accurately detecting blocks eligible for threshold relaxation under near-zero false alarms, and translating prediction results into executable write policies.
The main contributions are as follows:

(1) For failure prediction, this thesis analyzes proximal B-group mutation precursors on the Alibaba Cloud public SMART dataset, proposes a hybrid labeling strategy to suppress label noise in silent segments, constructs short-term temporal mutation features by combining $W$-order lag features with weighted mutation features, and builds an ensemble of RF, XGB, and LGB base learners fused by sample-level Attention. Experiments follow calendar-month temporal splits and optimize thresholds on the validation set with disk-level $F_{0.5}$ as the primary metric. On MB1/MB2, the heterogeneous ensemble achieves three-round mean $F_{0.5}$/TPR of 0.921/0.738 and 0.490/0.187, respectively, with FPR kept at a low level.

(2) For lifetime enhancement, this thesis constructs lifespan labels from block-level Fault Bit Count (FBC) and converts block-level prediction results into a differentiated write strategy: conservative write limits are applied to weak blocks, while thresholds for strong blocks are moderately relaxed within safety boundaries. Experimental results show that, compared with a uniform PE-threshold policy, the proposed strategy increases cumulative writable amount by about 17\%.

(3) Based on the proposed failure prediction and lifetime enhancement methods, this thesis designs and implements an intelligent SSD operation and maintenance software system, forming a closed loop from warning generation to status management, and realizing key functions such as asset management, log management, fault records, and warning handling.

In summary, this thesis forms an integrated technical pipeline from failure prediction to lifetime optimization and system implementation. It improves failure identification capability and storage resource utilization without significantly increasing false alarms, and provides a practical reference for reliable SSD operation and cost optimization in data centers.

\textbf{Keywords}：solid-state drive; machine learning; failure prediction; lifetime enhancement; low false-positive-rate constraint
